# CareRoute product context

## Register

product

## Users

Hackathon reviewers demonstrate a fictional adult completing an appointment request and a clinic staff member reviewing that request. The intake user needs a short, understandable form; the staff user needs the original description, a traceable suggestion, and a clear next action. The prototype runs locally on a laptop, often projected in a well-lit judging room.

## Product Purpose

CareRoute demonstrates AI-assisted administrative routing, not diagnosis. It collects an invented description, urgency, structured warning answers and appointment preferences, then uses a local model and explicit policy to propose routine appointment, same-day human review, or emergency guidance. Staff can review, override and make a synthetic booking. The original assignment requests an intake form, transparent routing rules/model, test cases and a safety note.

## Brand Personality

Calm, direct and capable. The interface should make a working demonstration easy to follow. Plain task labels and honest states matter more than promotional claims. These qualities follow the original UI/workflow specification and the current user's request for a substantially better-looking, complete product.

## Anti-references

The user explicitly rejected the existing pale-blue, card-heavy interface. Avoid its oversized promotional heading, decorative hero illustration, long wall of forms, repeated panel outlines and developer settings competing with the main task. Avoid generic AI marketing language and unsupported accuracy badges.

## Design Principles

- Put the current task and its next action first.
- Make the request, AI suggestion, staff review and booking visibly distinct stages.
- Keep advanced demonstration settings available without letting them dominate intake.
- Show the real model's contribution through source excerpts and transparent routing factors.
- Preserve emergency visibility, truthful fallback and reversible staff review through visual changes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow the existing specification: keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, programmatic labels, text as well as color for status, accessible emergency announcements, mobile layout without horizontal overflow, readable type and reduced-motion support. Avoid claiming a formal accessibility conformance audit has been completed.
