# Safety note for submission

**CareRoute is a synthetic administrative prototype. Use newly invented information only. It does not diagnose, treat, certify safety, contact emergency services, provide a real clinic review service or make real appointments. If a situation may be an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately; do not wait for this form, an AI result or clinic review.**

The persistent notice and immediate local checks operate before submission. The bounded text detector covers specified examples, not all phrasing or emergencies. A negative detector result is not a medical clearance. In no-AI mode, unassessed ordinary narrative goes to human review. Missing/unclear information, invalid assessments and unsupported scope cannot silently establish routine eligibility. Appointment preferences cannot reduce urgency.

The deterministic policy and its example warnings, adult-only scope and review targets are illustrative. The team has not approved clinical rules, jurisdiction, supported populations/languages, staffed hours, alternate-care contacts, escalation ownership or acceptable error rates. These omissions block real clinical use. No country is inferred from the user's timezone.

All 44 records were newly invented. No real, copied or de-identified patient conversation was used as a fixture, external-model input or screenshot. Literature sources were read for methods and limitations; their underlying records were not imported. The synthetic checkbox is a user declaration, not a technical guarantee that arbitrary text contains no personal information. Do not test with real records.

Staff can inspect original text, actual rules, prior model/replay results, policy versions and corrections. Explanations are input/rule records, not generated medical reasoning. Vendor confidence is not clinical safety probability. An emergency downgrade requires a distinct supervisor; prior guidance remains in history. Persistence/delivery failures never produce false success claims. Late model responses cannot override a human takeover.

Final evaluation reveals a cost: all six routine reference examples in the scored no-AI subset were escalated for human review. Zero emergency misses on 13 visible invented examples is not evidence of zero risk, generalization or clinical readiness. Live-model performance, calibration, human comprehension, actual reviewer handling time, fairness and clinical outcomes were not measured. Mandatory staff presence alone is not proof of safety.

Future work requires approved local policy, independently labelled held-out synthetic cases, live-model experiments, accessible human workflow testing and adequate staffing before any authorized clinical evaluation. DECIDE-AI and FUTURE-AI provide reporting/governance guidance, not approval of this prototype.
