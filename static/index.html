Three administrative suggestions

Emergency guidance

Contact local emergency services immediately. No submission, AI response or clinic review is required.

Same-day human review

Urgent, unclear, incomplete or unsupported requests need a person. Coverage availability is shown separately.

Routine appointment

Available when a valid assessment meets the demonstration’s routine conditions, or after a staff decision. The no-AI pathway sends ordinary unreviewed text to human review. Booking remains separate.

What the system does—and its limits

  • Records original text, structured inputs, actual rules, assessment mode and staff changes.
  • Keeps urgency independent of information sufficiency and appointment preference.
  • Supports explicit staff overrides with reasons and protection against concurrent edits.
  • Uses a persistent emergency notice and immediate bounded checks. It cannot recognize every emergency in free text.
  • Labels replay, no-AI, live and degraded behavior accurately. Saved examples are not live model results.
  • Does not diagnose, provide treatment, automatically book appointments or contact emergency services.

Real inference, on your computer

A small local language model reads each description and returns structured judgments about intent, warning signs, missing information and conflicts. It selects exact excerpts for the staff handoff.

Deterministic checks run independently. The model cannot override an immediate warning or book an appointment. If it is unavailable, the request goes to human review.

Not approved for clinical use

This policy is illustrative. Real use requires a team-approved policy, explicit jurisdiction, supported populations, staffed coverage, escalation contacts, accepted error thresholds and further evaluation.

All demo notifications are simulated. No patient, clinic or emergency service receives a message.
